What's costing bookings today

A walk through thewanderlustexplorers.com turned up these issues. The red ones make the site look unfinished, and a traveller about to pay ₹48,000 notices that.

CRIT

Test coupon is live

Offer cards show code TEST00001KA to every visitor.

CRIT

Dummy fares on top routes

DEL→GOI shows ₹111,111, BOM→BKK ₹444,444. Unknown codes like DED, YXX, CIR.

CRIT

Placeholder copy in public

"Monsoon Escape Offer" reads Add your content.

CRIT

Copy-pasted testimonials

Two reviews by different people have the same text word for word, and two of three reviewers are labelled "Agent".

WARN

Offer overload

About 40 offer cards, many repeated (Radisson ×4, "₹2000 OFF" ×5). Nobody scrolls past the third.

WARN

Generic white-label look

Same layout as hundreds of OTA templates. Nothing says who you are or why to book with you over MakeMyTrip.

WARN

Stale blog and dates

Last posts Apr 2025; "recent searches" stuck on 12-04-2025. Titles are truncated mid-word.

WARN

Small polish

"Premimum Economy", "assitance", uneven currency formats, emoji in headline tickers.

The big idea
Not another booking engine. Your travel crew.

The big OTAs win on price and inventory. You can win on people: a real Indian team on the phone (7737930736), visas handled end to end, group trips planned for you. The site should sell that crew, then let the booking engine do the rest.

Search

Mood board search

Passport stamps

Every trip stamps a digital passport. Five stamps unlock a free visa fee or airport transfer. Profiles become a travel diary.

↳ Repeat bookings
