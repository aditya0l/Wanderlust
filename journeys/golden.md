## Overview

Visit the ancient cities of Delhi, Agra, and Jaipur, collectively known as the Golden Triangle, and create some unforgettable memories along the way. Immerse yourself in the diverse culture, tradition, and history of India, by exploring these destinations that boast a mesmerizing fusion of Mughal, Persian, and Hindu architecture. In Delhi, you will see a vibrant mix of modernity and tradition, and numerous historical monuments such as Red Fort, Jama Masjid, and Qutub Minar. In Agra, the iconic Taj Mahal, one of the Seven Wonders of the World, awaits you. While in Jaipur, the Pink City, you will encounter magnificent forts, palaces, and colorful markets. With your 5 days Delhi Agra Jaipur tour package, indulge in the fun and excitement that these cities have to offer.

## Day-by-day itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Delhi

Arrive at New Delhi Railway station/Airport and get transferred to your hotel for check-in. Later, self-explore the city wherein you can visit attractions like Akshardham Temple, Chandni Chowk, etc. Head back to the hotel at your own pace for an overnight stay.

### Day 2: Delhi Sightseeing Tour

Get assisted from the hotel for a historical sightseeing tour of Delhi and explore the famous Red Fort, Raj Ghat, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, etc. Also, admire the beautiful Lotus Temple, followed by a visit to Jantar Mantar. Later, board your transfer back to the hotel for an overnight stay.

### Day 3: Arrival in Agra · Sikandra & Taj Mahal Sightseeing Tour

Check-out and get transferred to Agra. Upon arrival, check-in at the pre-booked hotel and later, get assisted for an Agra sightseeing tour. Visit Akbar's Tomb at Sikandra, followed by a visit to the Taj Mahal. Later, get transferred back to the hotel for an overnight stay.

### Day 4: Arrival in Jaipur · AMER Fort Sightseeing Tour

Check-out and get transferred to Jaipur. Upon arrival, check-in at the hotel and later, get assisted for an Amer Fort sightseeing tour. Explore and walk through different sections to understand about the history of the Fort. Then, board your transfer back to the hotel for an overnight stay.

### Day 5: Half-Day Jaipur Sightseeing Tour

Get assited for a Jaipur sightseeing tour and visit attractions like Hawa Mahal, City Palace, etc. Then, get transferred to the hotel and head to self-explore the city wherein you can visit Albert Hall Museum, Chokhi Dhani, etc. Head back to the hotel on your own for an overnight stay.

### Day 6: Departure Day

After breakfast, check-out and get transferred to the Delhi airport/Railway Station. This is where your trip comes to an end.
