Jaipur is the capital and the biggest city of the Indian territory of Rajasthan. Jaipur is called the Pink City, because of the popular shading plan of its structures. It is found 268 km from the national capital of New Delhi. Jaipur was established in 1727 by the Rajput ruler Jai Singh the leader of Amer, after whom the city is named. Jaipur is a significant visitor goal in India framing a piece of the Golden Triangle. Jaipur Exhibition and Convention Center (JECC) in Rajasthan's greatest show and display focus. Guest attractions incorporate the Albert Hall Museum, Hawa Mahal, Jal Mahal, City Palace, Amer Fort, Jantar Mantar, Nahargarh Fort, Birla Mandir, Galtaji, Govind Dev Ji Temple, Garh Ganesh Temple, Moti Dungri Ganesh Temple, The Jantar Mantar observatory and Amer Fort are one of the World Heritage Sites. Hawa Mahal is a five-story pyramidal formed landmark. Raj Mandir is a remarkable film lobby in Jaipur.

Ranthambhore is a national park in northern India, at first covering 282 km² at the time it was framed. Ranthambhore was set up as the Sawai Madhopur Game Sanctuary in 1955 by the Government of India and was proclaimed one of the Project Tiger holds in 1973. Ranthambhore turned into a national park on 1 November 1980. In 1984, the neighboring timberlands were announced the Sawai Man Singh Sanctuary and Keladevi Sanctuary. In 1992, the Tiger Reserve was extended to incorporate the connecting Keladevi Sanctuary in the north and Sawai Mansingh haven toward the south alongside different backwoods.

Meal : Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner.

### Day 3: JAIPUR

After breakfast, full day city tour of Albert Hall Museum, Jaipur Zoological Park, Birla Temple & World Trade Center. Dinner & overnight stay.

### Day 4: Ranthambore

After breakfast, drive to Ranthambore. On arrival lunch followed by hotel check in. Later on Canter Safari at Ranthambore National Park. Evening back to hotel. Rajasthani Cultural Show. Dinner & overnight stay.
